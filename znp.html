Zinc Phosphides

This page covers monoclinic ZnP₂ and Zn₃P₂, two zinc phosphides of interest for photovoltaic and optoelectronic applications.

Overview

Zinc phosphides are earth-abundant semiconductors of interest as photovoltaic absorbers. Monoclinic zinc diphosphide (ZnP₂) is an emerging absorber candidate, while Zn₃P₂ has a longer history in photovoltaic research.
