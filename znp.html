Zinc Phosphides

This page highlights two zinc phosphides relevant to photovoltaic and optoelectronic applications, with crystal structure, photoluminescence, band gap, and literature information.

Overview

Zinc phosphides are earth-abundant semiconductors of interest for photovoltaic absorbers. Monoclinic zinc diphosphide (ZnP₂) is an emerging absorber candidate, while Zn₃P₂ has a longer history in photovoltaic research.
